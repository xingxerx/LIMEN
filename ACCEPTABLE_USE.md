# Acceptable Use Policy

LIMEN is released under the Elastic License 2.0 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
In addition to the terms of that license, use of LIMEN — including its
source code, compiled artifacts, documentation, and any derivative works —
is subject to the restrictions below.

## Prohibited Uses

You may not use LIMEN, or permit it to be used, for any of the following:

1. **Weapons development.** Designing, simulating, optimizing, or
   manufacturing weapons of any kind, including conventional, nuclear,
   chemical, biological, or autonomous weapons systems.

2. **Mass surveillance.** Building or operating systems intended for the
   bulk, indiscriminate, or warrantless surveillance of individuals or
   populations, including communications interception, biometric tracking
   at scale, or social-credit-style monitoring systems.

3. **Unauthorized cryptanalysis.** Attacking, breaking, or undermining
   cryptographic systems, keys, or communications without the explicit,
   documented authorization of the system or data owner. This includes
   use against civilian, commercial, or critical-infrastructure
   cryptographic systems outside of a lawful, authorized security
   assessment.

4. **Attacks on critical infrastructure.** Disrupting, degrading, or
   gaining unauthorized access to power grids, water systems, financial
   systems, healthcare systems, transportation networks, or other critical
   infrastructure.

5. **Any use prohibited by applicable law,** including export-control and
   sanctions regimes applicable to quantum computing and dual-use
   cryptographic technology.

## Authorized Security Research

Nothing in this policy prohibits legitimate, authorized security research,
penetration testing, or defensive cryptanalysis conducted with the
explicit permission of the system owner, or academic research into
cryptographic primitives that does not target real-world systems without
authorization.

## Enforcement

Use of LIMEN in violation of this policy is a violation of the license
grant in [LICENSE](LICENSE) and will result in termination of your rights
to use the software, in addition to any other remedies available to the
licensor.

## Reporting

If you become aware of a violation of this policy, please report it to
the project maintainers.
